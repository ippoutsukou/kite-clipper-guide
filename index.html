WHY KITE-CLIPPER

Less time sorting.
More time riding.

The best moment is brief. Finding it in hours of footage is not.

Watching every minute again after a session is exhausting. kite-clipper uses AI for the first pass, then keeps review, tagging, and trimming in one clear workflow.

It does not turn AI loose to make a finished edit. It helps you miss fewer moments and spend less time getting to the decisions that matter.

ABOUT AUTO PICK

When AI finds no clips,
there is usually a reason.

Works best

The rider is reasonably large in frame

When kiteboarding movement is visible at a useful scale, Auto Pick can suggest highlights more reliably.

More difficult

Distant, ultra-wide, or unrelated footage

If the subject is only a few pixels tall, the app may return zero clips instead of padding the results with weak suggestions.

Zero suggestions is not a dead end. Select thumbnails yourself, then tag and export them in the same workflow.

FOR TESTERS

Your first-test
checklist

This order makes it easier to tell an app issue from footage that is difficult for the model.

  1. 1
    Start with a short kiteboarding video

    Try 10–30 seconds of footage where the rider is easy to see.

  2. 2
    Match the filming style

    For a tripod or stationary camera, choose Kiteboarding (Fixed Camera).

  3. 3
    Run AI Scoring, then Auto Pick

    Wait for scoring to finish before asking the app to suggest ranges.

  4. 4
    If it finds nothing, try manual selection

    Tap thumbnails in the grid and confirm that you can export the range.

  5. 5
    Note what you were doing

    Device, video length, filming distance, and the screen you were on all help us investigate.

FAQ

Common questions, answered

Is something broken if Auto Pick finds no clips?＋

Not necessarily. Auto Pick may return few or no suggestions when a rider appears very small in wide-angle footage, or when the footage is not kiteboarding. You can still select thumbnails yourself and export clips in exactly the same way.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?＋

No. Thumbnail generation, AI scoring, and trimming all happen on your iPhone or iPad. No account is required.

Will exporting reduce the video quality?＋

No. kite-clipper passes through the ranges you choose without recompressing the video, preserving the source quality and bitrate.

Can I add music or captions too?＋

kite-clipper is focused on finding and trimming the best moments. Add music, captions, and color adjustments in your usual editing app after export.
